FinTrack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

FinTrack ("the app") is a personal expense tracker and ledger for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

In short: your financial records, SMS-derived transactions and ledger contacts stay on your phone. FinTrack has no account system and no server of its own, and it does not upload your data anywhere. The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob, which shows ads.

1. Information stored on your device

The following is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Transactions you add (amount, category, note, date, income or expense).
  • Ledger contacts and entries you add (name, optional phone number, amounts you gave or got, notes).
  • Profile and settings you enter (name, optional email and phone, currency, monthly budget, recurring rules).
  • App lock PIN, stored in Android's encrypted secure storage. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; the app never receives your biometric data.

The developer cannot see or access any of this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

2. SMS messages

If you grant the SMS permission, FinTrack reads recent bank and payment SMS messages on your phone to detect transactions (amount, date, debit or credit, and merchant or bank name) so you can add them with one tap. It also notices new bank SMS so it can show how many are waiting to be imported.

  • SMS messages are processed entirely on your device.
  • SMS content is never uploaded, shared, or sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Only the transaction details you choose to save are kept, as normal transactions.
  • SMS access is optional. You can deny or revoke it at any time in Android Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

FinTrack shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version), and
  • ad interactions (impressions and taps).

FinTrack does not give AdMob your transactions, SMS messages, ledger contacts or profile. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

4. Features you start yourself

  • Backup export: creates a backup file of your data and opens Android's share menu. Where it goes (for example Drive, email or local storage) is your choice. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Backup import: reads a backup file you pick, and only that file.
  • WhatsApp reminders: in the ledger, you can open WhatsApp with a pre-filled reminder message. Nothing is sent until you send it in WhatsApp.

5. Permissions

  • Read and receive SMS: to detect bank transactions, as described above. Optional.
  • Biometric / fingerprint: to unlock the app if you turn on app lock. Optional.
  • Internet and network state: used by Google AdMob to load ads.
  • Vibration: for touch feedback.

6. Data sharing and selling

The developer does not collect, sell, rent or share your personal or financial information. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob collects to show ads (section 3), and anything you choose to export or send yourself (section 4).

7. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add an app lock with a PIN or biometrics. No method of storage is completely secure, so keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

8. Children

FinTrack is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your choices

  • Edit or delete any transaction or ledger entry inside the app.
  • Delete all data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
  • Revoke SMS or other permissions in Android Settings at any time.
  • Control ad personalisation in your device's Google ad settings.

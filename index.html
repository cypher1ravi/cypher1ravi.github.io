Know where your money goes

Expenses, budgets, bank SMS and your khata in one private app for Android. No sign-up. Your data stays on your phone.

Coming soon on Google Play Privacy policy

The four buttons on FinTrack's Home screen are the whole idea: money in, money out, who owes whom, and what your bank already told you.

Record a transaction in seconds

Add income or an expense with an amount, a category and a note. Your balance for the month updates straight away.

  • Categories with icons for food, travel, bills, shopping and more
  • Recurring entries for rent, salary and subscriptions
  • Full history grouped by day, with search and filters

See what you spend, and stay on budget

Set a monthly budget and watch it fill as you spend. The breakdown shows which categories take the most, this month or across the year.

  • Budget progress on the Home screen
  • Spending by category for the month or the year
  • Yearly trend to compare months

A khata for money you lend and borrow

Keep a page for each friend, relative or customer. FinTrack keeps the running balance, so you always know who owes you and whom you owe.

  • Entries for money you gave and money you got
  • Totals for what you'll get and what you'll give
  • Send a payment reminder on WhatsApp in one tap

Let your bank SMS do the typing

FinTrack reads your bank and UPI messages on your phone and finds the transactions in them. Check each one and save it with a tap.

  • Amount, date and debit or credit filled in for you
  • Messages you've already saved are skipped
  • Optional: the rest of the app works without SMS access

Your money is nobody else's business

No account, no sign-up
Open the app and start. There's no login and no FinTrack server holding your records.
Everything stays on your phone
Transactions, ledger contacts and SMS are processed and stored on your device and never uploaded.
Locked when you want it
Protect the app with a PIN, fingerprint or face unlock, and export a backup file whenever you like.
Free, with ads
FinTrack shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads never see your transactions or messages. Read the privacy policy.
